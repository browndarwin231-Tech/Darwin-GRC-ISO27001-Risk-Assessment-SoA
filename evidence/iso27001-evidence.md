# ISO 27001 Risk Assessment Evidence

## Purpose

This folder contains supporting evidence for the simulated ISO 27001 risk assessment and Statement of Applicability project for CloudNova Technologies.

The evidence demonstrates how information security risks were identified, scored, treated, mapped to ISO 27001 controls, and reviewed for residual risk.

## Evidence Included

- ISO 27001 risk register
- Statement of Applicability
- Risk treatment plan
- Control justification documentation
- Residual risk review

## Key Assessment Areas

- Identity and Access Management
- Privileged Access
- Third-Party Risk
- Vulnerability Management
- Incident Response
- Logging and Monitoring
- Backup and Recovery
- Security Awareness
- Business Continuity

## Risk Treatment Approach

Risks are evaluated using likelihood and impact.

Treatment options include:

- Mitigate
- Accept
- Avoid
- Transfer

The project primarily uses mitigation for High and Medium risks.

## Residual Risk Review

Residual risk is reviewed after treatment to determine whether the remaining exposure is acceptable.

High inherent risks are reduced to Medium or Low residual risk where appropriate.

## Final Assessment

The project demonstrates how ISO 27001 risk management connects risk identification, control selection, treatment planning, evidence collection, and residual risk acceptance.
